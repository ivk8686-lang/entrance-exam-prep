PREPONE V6 — TOPIC RESOURCE COLUMN GUIDE
========================================

Syllabus column order:
A  SyllabusID
B  ExamID
C  Subject
D  Chapter
E  Topic
F  UnitOrder
G  ChapterOrder
H  TopicOrder
I  Weightage (optional)
J  SourceURL (optional)
K  Active
L  MCQ Quiz
M+ Other resource URL columns

Recommended M+ headers:
Video 1 | Video 2 | Video 3 | PDF 1 | PDF 2 | Website 1 | Website 2 | Audio 1 | Audio 2 | Image 1 | Image 2

You can add more columns after these, for example:
Video 4 | PDF 3 | PYQ PDF | Formula Sheet | Website 3 | Audio 3 | Image 3

Rules:
- Put only one URL in each resource cell.
- Empty resource cells are hidden automatically.
- Topic workspace is collapsed by default.
- Every resource viewer is collapsed by default.
- MCQ Quiz always displays last inside the topic, even though its Sheet column is L.
- Use HTTPS URLs.
- YouTube embeds use youtube-nocookie privacy-enhanced mode and PrepOne blocks pop-ups/top-page navigation in sandboxed iframes.
- YouTube may still serve advertisements inside its own player. PrepOne cannot remove cross-origin/provider-served in-player ads.
- For guaranteed ad-free video use a direct MP4/WebM file or an ad-free file host/Google Drive video URL you control.
